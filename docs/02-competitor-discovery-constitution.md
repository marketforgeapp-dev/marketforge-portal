MARKETFORGE COMPETITOR DISCOVERY & RANKING CONSTITUTION

Purpose

The competitor set exists to represent the businesses a consumer is most likely to choose instead of the onboarded business.

The competitor set does NOT exist to represent:

* The nearest businesses  
* The highest-ranked Google listings  
* The businesses with the most Google categories  
* The businesses with physical locations closest to the onboarded company

The competitor set should reflect actual consumer competition.

Core Principles

1. Consumer Choice First

Competitors should be selected based on which businesses consumers are most likely to choose as alternatives.

The purpose of competitor discovery is to identify businesses competing for the same customers.

Competitor discovery exists to support business objective execution.

The primary question is:

"If a consumer does not choose this business, who are they likely to choose instead?"

2. Service Area Over Physical Location

For local service businesses, service area overlap is more important than office proximity.

Consumers do not care where a company's office is located.

Consumers care whether the company will service their home.

3. One Brand \= One Competitor

Consumers think in brands, not Google Business Profile locations.

Multiple locations belonging to the same company should be deduplicated into a single competitor whenever possible.

4. High Consumer Awareness Matters

Large regional brands and highly visible companies should not be excluded simply because they are farther away.

If consumers regularly recognize and consider a company, that company should likely appear in the candidate set.

5. Service Relevance Matters

Competitors should offer similar services.

A company that performs the same work is generally a stronger competitor than a nearby company that performs different work.

6. Discovery and Ranking Are Separate Problems

Discovery should maximize recall.

Ranking should maximize relevance.

Do not prematurely remove competitors during discovery.

7. Trust Test

A business owner should immediately recognize most competitors shown.

If obvious competitors are missing, trust in the platform decreases.

8. Consumer Competition Is More Important Than Google Competition

The objective is to model consumer decision-making.

The objective is not to replicate Google Maps rankings.
