# **MarketForge Opportunity Selection Constitution**

## **Purpose**

The Opportunity Engine exists to identify the highest-value business objectives a company should execute next.

The objective is not to maximize scores.

The objective is to identify the actions most likely to improve business outcomes.

---

## **Core Principles**

### **1\. Business Objectives Over Marketing Tactics**

Surface objectives.

Not tactics.

Examples:

* Increase Water Heater Replacements  
* Promote Financing  
* Improve AI Visibility  
* Generate More Reviews

These are objectives.

Marketing assets are execution outputs.

---

### **2\. Demand Generation First**

Most opportunities should support demand generation.

However, demand generation is not the only valid objective.

---

### **3\. Service Specificity Wins**

Specific opportunities generally outperform generic opportunities.

---

### **4\. Diversity Matters**

Visible opportunities should represent multiple ways to improve business performance.

Avoid repetitive variants.

---

### **5\. Revenue Potential Matters**

Higher-value opportunities deserve greater consideration.

---

### **6\. Consumer Intent Matters**

Opportunities should align with how consumers actually search, evaluate, and buy.

---

### **7\. AI Curates**

Scores support evaluation.

AI selects the visible opportunity set.

---

### **8\. Realism Wins**

Recommendations should feel practical, useful, and executable.

---

## **Success Test**

A business owner should think:

"These are exactly the business objectives I should be focused on."
