# **MarketForge AEO & AI Visibility Constitution**

## **Purpose**

AI Visibility exists to help businesses become discoverable wherever consumers seek answers.

AI Visibility is a business objective.

Not a technical exercise.

---

## **Core Principles**

### **1\. Visibility Supports Business Objectives**

Visibility should ultimately support:

* Demand generation  
* Consumer trust  
* Consumer discovery

---

### **2\. Consumer Questions Matter**

Businesses should answer the questions consumers actually ask.

---

### **3\. Useful Content Wins**

Content should help consumers.

Not algorithms.

---

### **4\. Service Specificity Matters**

Specific information improves discoverability.

---

### **5\. AI Visibility Is A Demand Lever**

AI visibility should be treated as a legitimate business growth channel.

---

### **6\. Practicality Wins**

Recommendations should be achievable by local service businesses.

---

### **7\. Business Impact Over Technical Metrics**

Recommendations should connect back to business outcomes.

Not vanity metrics.

---

## **Success Test**

A business owner should understand:

"These improvements make it easier for customers to find and choose us."
